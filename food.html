Dining on Taniti

Taniti currently has 10 restaurants offering a variety of dining experiences, from local Tanitian cuisine to international flavors. Whether you're craving fresh local fish or familiar comfort food, you'll find options to satisfy your appetite.

Restaurant Options

Local Tanitian Cuisine

5 Restaurants

Experience authentic Tanitian flavors at five restaurants that serve mostly local fish and rice. These establishments offer the freshest local catch prepared in traditional Tanitian style, giving you a true taste of the island's culinary heritage.

American-Style Dining

3 Restaurants

For those craving familiar flavors from home, three restaurants serve American-style meals. These establishments offer comfort food and familiar dishes for visitors who want a taste of home during their island getaway.

Pan-Asian Cuisine

2 Restaurants

Enjoy diverse Asian flavors at two restaurants serving Pan-Asian cuisine. These establishments offer a variety of dishes representing different Asian culinary traditions, adding international flair to Taniti's dining scene.

Grocery & Convenience

Supermarkets

Two supermarkets provide full grocery shopping options for visitors staying in accommodations with kitchen facilities or those who prefer to prepare their own meals.

Smaller Grocery Stores

Two smaller grocery stores offer convenient shopping for basic necessities and food items in a more intimate setting.

24-Hour Convenience Store

One convenience store is open 24 hours a day for late-night needs, snacks, and emergency supplies.

Local Specialties

Don't miss the opportunity to try fresh local fish, a staple of Tanitian cuisine. The island's fishing heritage means you'll find the freshest catches prepared in traditional ways that highlight the natural flavors of the Pacific.

Holiday Dining Notice

Taniti enjoys a large number of national holidays throughout the year, and many restaurants will be closed on holidays. We recommend checking the holiday calendar when planning your dining experiences and making reservations accordingly.
