Adventure Awaits on Taniti

Top Attractions

Most visitors come to Taniti to experience these natural wonders

Beautiful Beaches

Enjoy both sandy and rocky beaches around the island. The white, sandy beaches that encircle Yellow Leaf Bay near Taniti City are particularly popular with tourists for their pristine beauty and native architecture backdrop.

Rainforest Exploration

Explore the lush tropical rainforests that cover much of Taniti. Take guided hikes through diverse ecosystems and discover the island's rich biodiversity in this unspoiled natural environment.

Volcano Visits

Visit Taniti's small, active volcano located in the mountainous interior. This unique geological feature offers spectacular views and an unforgettable experience for adventure seekers.

Adventure Activities

Chartered Fishing Tours

Experience the waters around Taniti with professional fishing guides. Learn about local fishing traditions while trying to catch the same fish that have sustained the island's economy for generations.

Snorkeling

Discover the underwater world around Taniti's varied coastline. The clear Pacific waters offer excellent visibility for exploring marine life around both sandy and rocky coastal areas.

Rainforest Zip-lining

Soar through the treetops of Taniti's lush tropical rainforest on exciting zip-line adventures. Experience the forest canopy from a thrilling new perspective.

Helicopter Rides

Take to the skies for breathtaking aerial views of Taniti's diverse terrain, from the volcanic mountains to the pristine beaches and everything in between.

Sightseeing & Tours

Taniti City

Spend time in Taniti City, where most tourists base themselves. Admire the native architecture and enjoy easy access to the beautiful white, sandy beaches of Yellow Leaf Bay.

Boat Tours

Explore Taniti from the water with comprehensive boat tours around the island. See the varied coastline, beaches, and harbor from a unique maritime perspective.

Bus Tours

Take guided bus tours to see all corners of the island comfortably. Learn about Taniti's history, culture, and natural features from knowledgeable local guides.

Local History Museum

Discover Taniti's rich cultural heritage and learn about the island's indigenous population, traditional fishing and agricultural practices, and historical development.

Entertainment & Nightlife

Merriton Landing - Entertainment Hub

Many entertainment activities are located in Merriton Landing, a rapidly developing area on the north side of Yellow Leaf Bay. This area is easy to explore on foot and offers a variety of modern entertainment options.

Pubs & Microbrewery

Visit several local pubs, including a unique microbrewery where you can sample locally crafted beers and enjoy the island's emerging craft beverage scene.

Dance Club

Experience Taniti's nightlife at the new dance club, where you can dance the night away and mingle with both locals and fellow travelers.

Movie Theater

Catch the latest films at Taniti's movie theater, perfect for a relaxing evening or escaping the tropical heat during the day.

Arcade

Enjoy classic and modern arcade games, perfect for families or anyone looking for some nostalgic entertainment.

Art Galleries

Explore local and regional art at several galleries showcasing the creativity and cultural expressions of Pacific island artists.

Bowling

Strike up some fun at Taniti's bowling alley, a great activity for groups, families, or rainy day entertainment.

Planning Your Activities

Getting Around

Taniti City is fairly flat and very walkable, making it easy to access many activities on foot. The Merriton Landing area is also designed for pedestrian exploration. For activities further afield, consider public buses (5am-11pm), private island buses, taxis in Taniti City, rental cars, or bike rentals (helmets required).

Important Planning Note

Taniti enjoys a large number of national holidays, and many tourist attractions and restaurants will be closed on holidays. Be sure to plan your activities accordingly and check holiday schedules when planning your visit.
